# Mita Lite — MiSide fan edition

This is an **unofficial, community-created fan project** inspired by the mood of MiSide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's creators.

## Visual direction

Original dark-purple/pink desktop artwork, soft geometric stars, Xfce dark theme and minimal animations for older laptops. The wallpaper is original vector art in this repository, not extracted game content.

## Asset policy

Do not bundle MiSide game files, character sprites, screenshots, logos, music, or other copyrighted assets without appropriate permission. Contributors can create their own original art or use appropriately licensed assets with attribution.

## User experience

The desktop must remain easy to exit and control. No forced startup messages, intrusive notifications, deceptive windows, or hidden background activity.

## Hardware

The Debian amd64 build is for both 64-bit Intel and AMD processors. Test the ISO in a disposable VM before physical installation.
